Technova is the annual tech fest of our college, held from 14 to 16 March. This year's theme is "Retro Future", mixing old-school gaming with futuristic technology.

The fest opens on 14 March at 9:30 AM with an inauguration in the Main Auditorium. The chief guest is Dr. Meera Kapoor, a robotics researcher. The opening keynote is titled "Building Robots at Home" and lasts 45 minutes.

Registration costs 200 rupees per participant for a single event and 350 rupees for the all-events pass. Teams of up to 4 members can register together and pay 600 rupees per team. Registration closes on 8 March at 5 PM and can be done at the front desk of the Admin Block or on the college website.

The main events are the Hackathon, the Robo Race, the Retro Gaming Arena, and Quiz Wars. The Hackathon runs for 24 hours starting at 10 AM on 14 March in Lab 3. The Robo Race takes place on 15 March in the Sports Ground. The Retro Gaming Arena is open all three days in the Seminar Hall.

The Hackathon winner gets 25,000 rupees and the runner-up gets 15,000 rupees. The Robo Race winner gets 10,000 rupees. The Quiz Wars winner gets a smartwatch and a trophy.

Food stalls will be set up in the Central Courtyard from 11 AM to 8 PM every day. The most popular stall is expected to be Byte Bites, which serves pizza and cold coffee. Water stations are available near every building.

Participants must carry their college ID card or a government ID. Outside laptops are allowed only for the Hackathon. Anyone found cheating in Quiz Wars will be disqualified immediately.

The fest ends on 16 March at 6 PM with a music night by the college band Static Signals, followed by the prize distribution. For help, contact the fest coordinator Arjun Rao at technova-help@example.com.
